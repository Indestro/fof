# Cold email to emerging managers — SF Tech Week, Oct 6-8 2026

**The premise: we are the only LP who can open with evidence rather than a request.** For most of these
managers we can name deals in their own book that appeared on our stealth-founder feed before they
invested. That is the whole email. No pitch, no ask beyond a meeting that is already scheduled by
geography.

**Rules**
- Value first, no ask in the first mail. The meeting is the only call to action, and Tech Week supplies
  the excuse.
- **Every named deal must be verified before sending.** One wrong date and the credibility is gone —
  which is the entire asset here. Check the founder's LinkedIn slug, the round date and the investor
  list against Crunchbase before the name goes in.
- If we have no convergence case for a manager, do not invent a softer version. Use variant B.
- One paragraph of us, maximum. They do not care yet.
