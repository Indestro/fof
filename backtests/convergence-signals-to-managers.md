third-party timestamped) x 350 companies the 9 backtest managers bought since Mar-2024 (638 founder
records from Crunchbase). Name-normalised match; lead time = investment date - signal week.

## Why this is the strongest card we have
It is not "we beat the managers" (unsupportable: our record starts Mar-2024, their unicorns were
bought 2021-23). It is convergence: the engine and the manager selection point at the same founders,
each verifiable, one of them timestamped inside documents delivered weekly to a third party (Wing)
that neither we nor they can retroactively edit. It also compounds — every future week adds rows.

## Limits
Name-matching only (no LinkedIn-slug confirmation on the portfolio side); Crunchbase founder coverage
is partial (638 founders across 350 companies); Wing triage/claims measure interest, not returns.
