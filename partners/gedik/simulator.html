If this fund had started in August 2024

Retrospective with real deal data under the SAME construction rules as the simulator above (tiered weights, 30% co-invest sleeve, fee drag): the signal-selected managers' actual 2024-vintage investments, marked to verified valuations · year 2 of 10 — far too early to judge, and shown because it disagrees with the model in places

Two findings that argue against our own model. (1) Tiering would have hurt in this window (1.51x vs 1.60x equal weight): the best 2024-vintage books were Wischoff (2.40x) and Quiet (2.66x), while JAM — our strongest pre-2024 performer — is at 0.96x on just 5 positions. Weights estimated on one period misfire on the next; the disciplined response is to start closer to equal weight and concentrate only as fresh evidence accrues, re-tiering at Fund II. (2) A co-invest sleeve chosen without skill would have hurt too: 12 positions drawn at random from the same companies return ~1.27x median even with the no-fee advantage, pulling the portfolio to 1.49x. The simulator above credits co-invest with precursor-informed selection; this window cannot prove that skill, so treat the sleeve's modeled premium as an assumption, not a finding.
