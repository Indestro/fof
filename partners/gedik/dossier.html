The founding myth: from zero, under a borrowed name — and there are two Gedik families

Erhan Topaç (b. 1953; Boğaziçi mechanical engineering MS + doctorate in mathematical modeling; Eczacıbaşı 1979–89, Pepsi marketing 1989–90) founded the brokerage in 1991 with TL 45,000 equity, inside the orbit of — and named for — Halil Kaya Gedik's welding group (Gedik Holding's own 2012 page: "Gedik Investment as a member of GEDIK HOLDING"). Ownership later consolidated under Topaç (today 81% of Inveo with 15× voting A-shares he holds in full); the welding family's exit date is unpublished. The Gedik Eğitim Vakfı and Gedik University belong to the welding family (Hülya Gedik) — Erhan served as university trustee 2010–21, and Onur's June keynote happened at their summit. Two families, one brand, sustained alliance.

His self-narrative, verbatim at the 2022 handover: "Biz bu yolculuğa... mevcut yatırımcı tabanı olmadan, sıfırdan başladık... Sermaye piyasalarına adanmış bir hayatımız var." ("We started from zero, with no investor base... Ours is a life devoted to the capital markets.") His register is a valuation engineer's: minority-IPO discounts of "at least 20–30%", walking from mispriced mandates, "menzillerini hep uzun tuttuk" (we always kept the horizon long), and the evangelist line "ev ev dolaşıp satmamız lazım" (we must sell this door to door). Identity: "the school of the capital markets" — he counts people trained, not AUM.

Succession was engineered in stages — the stage was given, the state was kept

Onur enters 2008 at 18 as website manager; builds the IB division 2013; inherits the VC shell (HUB GSYO — which Erhan himself chaired 2006–15) in 2015; CEO Nov 2017 under a KAP filing framed as "kurumsallaşma" (corporatization — institutional language clothing a dynastic act); chairman Apr 2022, with the handover release crediting him 14× profit and 6× equity in his five CEO years — the heir's proof-of-worth, published. Erhan retains: 81% of the holding, all 15×-vote A-shares, and chairs Misyon Yatırım Bankası — note that the newest, most future-facing asset (digital custody, Avalanche/Chainlink) stayed with the father. Only Erhan and Onur appear in Inveo's reports; no other children are published.

A Jungian read of the dyad — interpretation, grounded in the record

Status: analytical interpretation built on verified biography; nothing in this section is a sourced fact about their inner lives.

Erhan is a senex who never stopped building. The mathematical-modeling doctorate and the valuation-lecture register mark a thinking-sensation psyche: control through structure. His break came at ~37 — Jung's "afternoon of life" turn — leaving salaried houses to found his own, but under another family's name: identity earned through work while name-glory was deferred for three decades. The 15×-vote architecture is the senex's ring: the crown passes, the sword stays. His myth ("from zero, no investor base") is self-made-outsider — and his devotional language ("a life devoted") makes the firm a vocation, not an asset. That he kept the bank — the frontier asset — shows a senex with unspent puer energy: he does not experience himself as finished.

Onur's whole psyche is organized around earning what he was given. Entering at 18 as website manager is an apprenticeship ritual — humility performed and real; the credential accumulation (financial-engineering MSc, SPK certificates, licensed angel at 25) is compensation against the inheritance shadow. His individuation project is venture: the one franchise where the father's founder-myth doesn't blot the sun — and even there, his pattern is to take a paternal shell (HUB) and transmute it into something the father couldn't have built (Inveo Ventures: AI thesis, external LPs, the global conference circuit). The 2020 renaming of the holding — shedding the borrowed "Gedik" name in the son's CEO era — reads as the son completing the father's own deferred individuation. His keynote register (nation-scale: "bring the AI-first HQs to Turkey") signals the scale-jump ambition: the father built a Turkish institution; the son wants a global one.

Why the Delaware FoF is psychologically ideal: it sits outside the father's kingdom (new soil, new law), inside the son's own franchise (VC), in the future-domain (AI), on a horizon that speaks the father's core value ("menzil uzun" — keep the horizon long), with a partner from outside the father's world. It is differentiation without rebellion — the rare move a controlling senex can bless, because it extends his myth (start from zero, build long, institutionalize) instead of negating it. Practical consequences: expect enthusiasm-then-pause — the pause is not doubt, it is the upward-sale moment to the holder of the A-shares; hand Onur father-ready material (governance, capital protection, long-horizon language — quote Erhan's own values back to the family). And never frame Gedik as a small player buying access; frame it as the institution that built Turkish capital markets from zero, now routing Turkish capital correctly into the global AI cycle — the father's myth fused with the son's phrase. Haluk completes the court as the faithful steward; win him with precision, and the story carries itself upward.
