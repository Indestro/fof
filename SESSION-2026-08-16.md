# Session log — 2026-08-16

## What we learned

**1. Tier one now has one definition.** Emrah's hand-picked 29 (sheet `18xv96q…`) is canonical, and
Yasin's `has_tier_1_investor` flag agrees with it on **98.7% of 40,000 rounds**. Our bottom-up
measured list agrees on 20/29 — the gaps are informative, not errors (Coatue/Iconiq/IVP/Thrive are
growth firms an A/B-lead list cannot see; Caffeinated is a genuine omission).

**2. Every preceder ranking in circulation measures cheque volume.** Yasin's top-preceder sheet has
three columns carrying one number: `Success Rate` divides everyone by the same constant 2,610, and
`Quality Score` = n²/2,610. Re-ranked against a real base rate (5.88%), YC falls 1→25, Alumni 2→26,
Gaingels 9→38, while SV Angel rises 37→1 and A* 27→5. Our own `frontrunner_roster_v0.json` had the
identical flaw.

**3. Our manager shortlist mostly does not survive measurement.** On the unified base, Mei Zuo is
0/4 entries (unassessable), Day One 1.00x on 34, Mischief 1.76x on 28. Four candidates — Humba,
Chemistry, Category, Theory — have no seed entries in the 2019-23 window at all. The names that
clear base by >4 standard errors are ones the sweep found: Chapter One, Silent Ventures, Abstract,
plus SV Angel and A*.

**4. Being early only pays in some sectors, and we can measure which.** Head start for the feeder
layer runs from +270 days (energy) to −181 (crypto). Aerospace is the best extension: +113 days,
tier-one deal count +46%, median cheque $20M→$50M, 7 new unicorns in 2026. Robotics and crypto have
*negative* head start — the top firms are at seed first, so there is nothing to front-run. Our home
lane is compressing fastest: infrastructure seed→A fell 481→283 days in one year.

**5. Our sourcing has a lane bias that contaminates one metric but not the other.** The manager
front-run table is lane-neutral (full round corpus). The **convergence** metric is Wing-conditional
by construction — Pebblebed's 1-of-55 measured what we don't look at, not Pebblebed.

**6. Yasin's Mongo is the unlock.** 199,620 parsed rounds + 9.8M LinkedIn profiles with
`experiences`, joinable to our signal log at a 99% rate. It obsoletes the Crunchbase scraping path.

**7. "Find a hit and load on" is wrong at $40M.** Our edge is timing, not discovery; access is
bought through the fund sleeve; 3 positions carry a 39% chance of losing money; and our own backtest
says concentrating into conviction managers *lost*. The doctrine is a barbell — broad at the fund
layer, concentrated at the co-invest layer — and the FoF's job is to buy the right to load on later.

**8. The model was wrong in eight places and the page in twelve.** See
`intel/model-corrections-2026-08-16.md`. The two that mattered most: vintage risk modelled as a
thin-tailed lognormal (no 2000 or 2021 possible), and manager premiums taken from in-sample maxima
with no persistence haircut.
